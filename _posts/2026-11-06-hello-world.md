## Adding a post

New posts are Markdown files in the `_posts/` folder, named
`YYYY-MM-DD-some-title.md`, with a little header at the top:

```markdown
---
layout: post
title: "My post title"
description: "Optional one-line summary shown on the blog index."
---

Write the post here in Markdown.
```

Push it to the `master` branch and GitHub rebuilds the site automatically.
